## 🎯 Tips for Promotion

### Best Times to Post:
- **Reddit**: Weekday mornings (9-11 AM EST)
- **Hacker News**: Weekday mornings (avoid Friday)
- **Product Hunt**: Launch on Tuesday-Thursday
- **Twitter**: Weekdays 12-3 PM EST

### Hashtag Strategy:
**High Volume:** #productivity #notetaking #app
**Medium Volume:** #studytools #organization #webapp
**Niche:** #notetakingapp #digitalnotebook #studentlife

### Don't Be Salesy:
- Focus on solving problems
- Share your journey/story
- Ask for feedback
- Engage with comments
- Be authentic

### Track Everything:
- Use UTM parameters: `?utm_source=reddit&utm_campaign=launch`
- Monitor which channels bring traffic
- Double down on what works

---

**Remember**: Consistency > Perfection. Post regularly, engage authentically, and build your community! 🚀
